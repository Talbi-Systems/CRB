Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

1. Who We Are

CRB ("we", "us", "our") operates this careers portal. For any privacy-related questions, contact us at privacy@crb.gg.

2. What Data We Collect

When you submit an application, we collect:

  • Roblox username
  • Discord username
  • Age range (not exact age)
  • Time zone
  • Availability
  • Your answers to application questions
  • Preferred support language (if applicable)

3. Why We Collect It

We process your data solely to review your application and communicate with you about the recruitment process. Our legal basis is your explicit consent, which you provide by checking the consent box before submitting.

4. How Long We Keep It

We retain application data for a maximum of 6 months from the date of submission, after which it is permanently deleted. Successful applications may be retained longer if you join the team, in which case a separate agreement applies.

5. Who Has Access

Your data is accessible only to authorized CRB HR personnel for the purpose of reviewing your application. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.

6. Your Rights

Under the GDPR and UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Withdraw your consent at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@crb.gg. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures including HTTPS encryption, hashed HR credentials, and access controls to protect your data.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.
